# Manage Stakeholders Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to identify stakeholders, analyze interests and influence, and execute engagement plans.

## Purpose
The **Manage Stakeholders** competency demonstrates the ability to identify, analyze, and build productive relationships with all individuals and groups affected by the project.

## Performance Criteria
1. Identify all direct and indirect stakeholders.
2. Assess stakeholder power, interest, and potential impact.
3. Author the [[Stakeholder Management Plan]] and [[Stakeholder Register]].
4. Execute proactive engagement and measure shifts in stakeholder support.
