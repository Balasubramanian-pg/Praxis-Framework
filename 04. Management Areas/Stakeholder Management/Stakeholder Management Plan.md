# Stakeholder Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Stakeholder Management Plan defines how stakeholders will be identified, analyzed, and engaged throughout the life cycle.

## Purpose
The **Stakeholder Management Plan** sets out the policy and procedures for identifying individuals and groups affected by or having influence over the project or programme, and designing engagement strategies to win and sustain their commitment.

## Core Structure
1. **Policy**:
   - Governance structure for stakeholder relationships.
   - Principles of transparent and empathetic communication.
   - Roles of the Sponsor, Project Manager, and Communications Lead.
2. **Procedure**:
   - **Identification**: Techniques to locate hidden or indirect stakeholders.
   - **Assessment**: Analyzing interest, power/influence, and current vs. required attitude.
   - **Engagement**: Designing tailored communication strategies and messages.
3. **Artifact Standards**:
   - Format and maintenance rules for the [[Stakeholder Register]] and [[Communication Plan]].

## Timing & Ownership
- **Authored By**: Project/Programme Manager in the [[Definition Process]].
- **Approved By**: Project Sponsor.
- **Executed In**: The [[Delivery Process]] across all tranches/stages.
