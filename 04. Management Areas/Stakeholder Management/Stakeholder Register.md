# Stakeholder Register

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Stakeholder Register documents all stakeholders, their interests, influence levels, and tailored engagement approaches.

## Purpose
The **Stakeholder Register** is the primary repository for recording stakeholder attributes, expectations, influence, and strategic engagement plans.

## Key Fields
- **Stakeholder Name & Role**: Internal or external group/individual.
- **Interest Area**: What they care about (outputs, benefits, disruption, financial return).
- **Influence / Power Level**: High, medium, or low ability to affect project outcomes.
- **Current Attitude**: Supportive, neutral, opposed, or unaware.
- **Target Attitude**: Desired stance required for project success.
- **Key Concerns & Expectations**: Specific needs or perceived risks.
- **Primary Communication Channel**: Preferred frequency and medium (email, workshop, 1-on-1).
- **Relationship Lead**: Assigned team member responsible for maintaining contact.

## Usage
- Created in the [[Identification Process]] / [[Definition Process]].
- Actively consulted when issuing [[Progress Report]]s or planning stage transitions.
