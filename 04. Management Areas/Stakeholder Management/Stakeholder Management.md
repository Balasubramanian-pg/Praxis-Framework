# Stakeholder Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Maturity benchmarks for identifying, analyzing, and engaging stakeholders.

## Overview
Assesses an organization's capability to manage relationships with stakeholders.

## Benchmarks
- **Level 1**: Ad-hoc communication; stakeholders managed only when issues arise.
- **Level 2**: Systematic stakeholder identification, [[Stakeholder Register]], and [[Communication Plan]].
- **Level 3**: Consistent stakeholder engagement frameworks embedded across all projects and programmes.
- **Level 4**: Quantitative feedback metrics, sentiment tracking, and engagement effectiveness scoring.
- **Level 5**: Proactive co-creation, collaborative governance, and continuous communication optimization.
