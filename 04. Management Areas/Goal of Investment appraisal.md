# Goal of Investment Appraisal

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Core goals and analytical methodologies for conducting project and programme investment appraisals.

## Goals of Investment Appraisal
The goal of **Investment Appraisal** is to determine whether the financial and non-financial return of a project justifies the expenditure and risk.

## Key Objectives
1. Provide a quantifiable basis for comparing candidate initiatives.
2. Calculate payback periods, Net Present Value (NPV), and Internal Rate of Return (IRR).
3. Evaluate the sensitivity of returns to changes in market conditions and cost overruns.
4. Support the Sponsor and governing board in go/no-go investment decisions.
