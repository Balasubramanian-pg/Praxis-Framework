# Financial Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Capability maturity attributes and benchmarks for organizational financial management.

## Overview
Defines the organizational capability benchmarks for **Financial Management** and cost control.

## Maturity Levels
- **Level 1**: Basic accounting without formal project cost baselining; frequent budget surprises.
- **Level 2**: Standardized [[Finance Management Plan]], cost breakdown structures, and periodic variance tracking.
- **Level 3**: Enterprise-wide financial integration, standardized estimating models, and formal cash flow modeling.
- **Level 4**: Statistical analysis of cost performance, Earned Value analysis, and probabilistic risk contingency modeling.
- **Level 5**: Continuous financial process refinement, value engineering, and strategic capital optimization.
