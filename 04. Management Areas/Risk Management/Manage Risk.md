# Manage Risk Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to identify, assess, plan responses for, and monitor risks across the life cycle.

## Purpose
The purpose of the **Manage Risk** competency is to demonstrate the practical ability to implement effective risk management in accordance with the [[Risk Management Plan]].

## Performance Criteria
1. Define the risk management approach and prepare the risk plan.
2. Identify threats and opportunities using structured techniques.
3. Assess the probability, impact, and proximity of identified risks.
4. Plan, evaluate, and assign cost-effective response strategies.
5. Maintain the [[Risk Register]] and report risk exposure to stakeholders.
