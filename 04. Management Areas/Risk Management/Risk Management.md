# Risk Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Organizational capability maturity benchmarks for proactive risk management.

## Overview
Benchmarks the maturity of an organization's risk management practices.

## Maturity Stages
- **Level 1**: Risks identified reactively after they occur; lack of formal register.
- **Level 2**: Structured [[Risk Management Plan]] and [[Risk Register]] used on all major initiatives.
- **Level 3**: Standardized organizational risk taxonomy, probability/impact guidelines, and risk ownership culture.
- **Level 4**: Quantitative Monte Carlo simulations, schedule/cost risk integration, and risk-adjusted portfolios.
- **Level 5**: Agile risk responses, proactive opportunity exploitation, and strategic resilience engineering.
