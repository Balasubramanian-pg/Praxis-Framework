# Risk Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Risk Management Plan sets out how risk management will be conducted across the project, programme, or portfolio.

## Purpose
The **Risk Management Plan** (RMP) defines the specific policies, procedures, tools, techniques, and responsibilities for identifying, assessing, and responding to risks within the initiative. It ensures that the approach to risk is tailored to the project's scale, complexity, and risk appetite.

## Structure & Key Components
1. **Policy**:
   - **Objectives**: Aims of risk management for this project/programme.
   - **Risk Appetite & Tolerances**: Acceptable thresholds for cost, schedule, and performance impacts.
   - **Roles & Responsibilities**: RACI matrix for risk owners, managers, and the sponsor.
2. **Procedure**:
   - **Identification**: Techniques used (PESTLE, SWOT, risk workshops, checklists).
   - **Assessment**: Probability-Impact matrices, expected monetary value (EMV), Monte Carlo simulation.
   - **Response Planning**: Threat strategies (avoid, transfer, reduce, accept) and opportunity strategies (exploit, share, enhance, accept).
   - **Review & Reporting**: Frequency of risk reviews, reporting cycles, and escalation mechanisms.
3. **Tools & Data**:
   - The format and repository for the [[Risk Register]].
   - Risk scoring criteria and probability/impact scales.

## Life Cycle Timing & Ownership
- **Created**: During the [[Definition Process]] by the Project/Programme Manager.
- **Approved**: By the Project/Programme Sponsor at the end of the Definition phase.
- **Maintained**: Reviewed at the end of each stage/tranche and updated if the context or risk environment changes.
