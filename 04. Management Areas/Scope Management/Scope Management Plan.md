# Scope Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Defines how scope will be captured, verified, structured into a WBS/PBS, and protected against unauthorized scope creep.

## Purpose
The **Scope Management Plan** defines the process for determining, validating, and controlling the work and deliverables of the initiative.

## Contents
- **Requirements Engineering**: Techniques for elicitation, prioritisation (MoSCoW), and validation.
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**: Standards for creating deliverable-oriented breakdown structures.
- **Scope Verification & Acceptance**: Formal procedures for deliverable inspection and sign-off.
- **Scope Baseline & Control**: Integration with change control procedures.
