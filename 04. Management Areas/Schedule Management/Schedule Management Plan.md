# Schedule Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Establishes the criteria, tools, and methodologies for developing, maintaining, and controlling project schedules.

## Purpose
The **Schedule Management Plan** sets out the approach to estimating durations, sequencing activities, choosing scheduling models, and monitoring progress against the timeline.

## Contents
- **Scheduling Methodology**: Critical Path Method (CPM), Agile timeboxing, or milestone charts.
- **Units of Measure & Estimating Accuracy**: Duration units (hours, days, sprints).
- **Schedule Baselining**: Rules for capturing and approving schedule baselines.
- **Maintenance & Updating**: Frequency of schedule updates and progress collection.

Directly connects to [[General Principles of Schedule Management]] and [[Time Scheduling]].
