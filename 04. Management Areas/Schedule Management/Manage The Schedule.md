# Manage The Schedule Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to plan, develop, optimize, and control project time schedules.

## Purpose
The **Manage The Schedule** competency certifies an individual's ability to sequence activities, estimate durations, construct network diagrams, and track time performance.

## Performance Criteria
1. Define activities and dependencies using Work and Product Breakdown Structures.
2. Estimate activity durations and apply resource constraints.
3. Calculate Critical Path, floats, and milestones.
4. Baseline the schedule and track actuals vs. planned progress.
5. Take corrective action when schedules deviate beyond tolerances.
