# Management Areas

> [!ABSTRACT] Section Overview
> In the Praxis Framework, **Management Functions** are the functional disciplines applied directly to deliver projects, programmes, and portfolios. Each management area encompasses its **General Principles**, **Management Plan**, **Registers/Artefacts**, **Individual Competencies**, and **Capability Maturity** attributes.
