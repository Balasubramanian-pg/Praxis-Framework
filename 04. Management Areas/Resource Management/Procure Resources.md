# Procure Resources Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to specify requirements, evaluate vendors, and establish commercial supply contracts.

## Purpose
The **Procure Resources** competency demonstrates the ability to obtain goods and services from external vendors fairly, cost-effectively, and compliantly.

## Performance Criteria
1. Formulate procurement packages, Statements of Work (SOW), and technical specs.
2. Conduct vendor tendering, request-for-proposals (RFP), and bid evaluations.
3. Negotiate contract terms, pricing models, and service level agreements (SLAs).
4. Award contracts and establish supplier governance mechanisms.
