# Mobilise Resources Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to onboard, organize, and equip personnel, facilities, and equipment for project execution.

## Purpose
The **Mobilise Resources** competency covers the operational setup of project infrastructure, team onboarding, tooling, and establishing the physical and virtual working environment.

## Performance Criteria
1. Secure agreed human resources from internal departments and external suppliers.
2. Conduct induction sessions covering project vision, tools, and quality standards.
3. Establish access to physical facilities, IT systems, and development environments.
4. Ensure safety, legal, and environmental compliance for operational readiness.
