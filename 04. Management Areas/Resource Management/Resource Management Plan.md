# Resource Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Defines how internal and external resources (people, equipment, materials, facilities) will be acquired, scheduled, and managed.

## Purpose
The **Resource Management Plan** provides the strategy for securing and managing the physical, technical, and human assets required to deliver the work.

## Structure
- **Resource Identification**: Skills matrix, capacity planning, equipment requirements.
- **Procurement Strategy**: Internal resource allocation vs. external contract engagement.
- **Mobilisation & Demobilisation**: Handover schedules and release dates.
- **Resource Smoothing & Levelling**: Guidelines for resolving resource over-allocation.

Maps directly to [[General Principles of Resource Management]] and [[Resource Scheduling]].
