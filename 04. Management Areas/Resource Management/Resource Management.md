# Resource Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Capability maturity attributes for securing, scheduling, and managing physical and human resources.

## Overview
Defines the maturity benchmarks for **Resource Management** across projects, programmes, and portfolios.

## Capability Attributes
- **Level 1**: Localized, reactive resource allocation; constant resource conflicts between departments.
- **Level 2**: Formal [[Resource Management Plan]] applied; stage-based resource schedules and procurement procedures.
- **Level 3**: Centralized resource management system; enterprise skills inventories and multi-project leveling.
- **Level 4**: Quantitative capacity planning, utilization metrics, and predictive supplier performance modeling.
- **Level 5**: Dynamic resource allocation responding to strategic shifts; proactive workforce capability development.
