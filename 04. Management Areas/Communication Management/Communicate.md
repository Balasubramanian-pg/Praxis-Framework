# Communicate Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to transmit clear, concise, and compelling messages across varied media and audiences.

## Purpose
The **Communicate** competency focuses on transmitting information effectively, listening actively, and confirming mutual understanding.

## Performance Criteria
1. Select appropriate communication channels and formats for diverse audiences.
2. Convey technical and managerial information clearly and concisely.
3. Practice active listening and elicit feedback from stakeholders.
4. Manage meetings and presentations efficiently.
