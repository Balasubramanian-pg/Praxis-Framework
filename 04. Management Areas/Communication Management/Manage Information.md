# Manage Information Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to establish information governance, document control, and record archiving.

## Purpose
The **Manage Information** competency covers the systematic capture, storage, protection, and retrieval of project data and documentation.

## Performance Criteria
1. Implement the [[Information Management Plan]].
2. Maintain document version control, access permissions, and naming conventions.
3. Ensure regulatory and corporate compliance for data security.
4. Archive project knowledge assets during closure.
