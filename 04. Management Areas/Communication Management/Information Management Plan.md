# Information Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Governs how project data, records, documentation, and institutional knowledge are captured, stored, secured, and distributed.

## Purpose
The **Information Management Plan** ensures that all project information is readily accessible, version-controlled, secure, and retained in compliance with organizational and statutory requirements.

## Structure
- **Storage & Architecture**: Document repositories, cloud folders, access controls.
- **Security & Privacy**: GDPR compliance, confidentiality tiers, data loss prevention.
- **Retention & Archiving**: Record retention periods and end-of-project archiving protocols.
