# Information Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Capability maturity benchmarks and assessment criteria for organizational information management.

## Overview
This note defines the capability maturity benchmarks for the **Information Management** function across levels 1 to 5.

## Maturity Benchmarks
- **Level 1 (Initial)**: Ad-hoc file storage, inconsistent naming conventions, and scattered documentation.
- **Level 2 (Managed)**: Structured [[Information Management Plan]] applied consistently; central document repositories established.
- **Level 3 (Defined)**: Organization-wide document taxonomies, security tiers, and automated version control.
- **Level 4 (Quantitatively Managed)**: Metrics on information retrieval time, compliance audits, and data accuracy.
- **Level 5 (Optimizing)**: Continuous optimization through semantic search, automated record management, and AI indexing.
