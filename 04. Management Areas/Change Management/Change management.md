# Change Management

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The management function that guides individuals and teams through organizational transitions to realize benefits.

## Overview
**Change Management** is the discipline that guides how we prepare, equip, and support individuals to successfully adopt change in order to drive organizational success and outcomes.

## Goals
1. Identify the organizational and cultural changes required to convert outputs into benefits.
2. Prepare the host business through engagement, leadership, and training.
3. Embed new practices into business-as-usual operations.
