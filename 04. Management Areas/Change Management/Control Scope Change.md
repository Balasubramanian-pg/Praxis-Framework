# Control Scope Change Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to capture change requests, evaluate impacts, and obtain authorized decisions before changing baselines.

## Purpose
The **Control Scope Change** competency ensures that changes to baselined products and specifications are disciplined, preventing uncontrolled scope creep.

## Performance Criteria
1. Log all change requests in the [[Change log]].
2. Analyze technical, schedule, cost, and benefit impacts of proposed changes.
3. Submit evaluations to the designated Change Authority (or Sponsor).
4. Communicate decisions and re-baseline project plans when approved.
