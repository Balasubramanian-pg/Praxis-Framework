# Change Management Artefact

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The structured document setting out organizational change policy, procedures, and transition plans.

## Overview
The **Change Management Artefact** (Change Management Plan) defines the approach to facilitating organizational adoption of project deliverables.

## Structure
- **Change Vision**: The desired future operational state.
- **Readiness Assessment**: Evaluation of stakeholder willingness and capability to change.
- **Intervention Plan**: Communications, coaching, training, and incentive programs.
- **Benefits Alignment**: Verification that organizational changes yield the planned business benefits.
