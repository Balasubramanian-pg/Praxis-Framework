# Manage Change Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to plan, lead, and embed organizational and cultural transitions required to realize business benefits.

## Purpose
The **Manage Change** competency focuses on the human, operational, and cultural shifts needed to turn project outputs into sustainable business outcomes and benefits.

## Performance Criteria
1. Assess organizational readiness and cultural barriers to change.
2. Formulate the change management strategy and transition plans.
3. Engage business leaders and operational teams throughout transition.
4. Embed new working practices, training, and operational support.
