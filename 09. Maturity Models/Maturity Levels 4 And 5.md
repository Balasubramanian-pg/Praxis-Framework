# Maturity Levels 4 And 5

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Characteristics and criteria for organizations operating at Quantitatively Managed (Level 4) and Optimizing (Level 5) maturity.

## Maturity Level 4: Quantitatively Managed
At Level 4, project and programme processes are measured and controlled using statistical and quantitative techniques:
- Quantitative objectives for quality and process performance are established.
- Sub-processes are chosen that significantly contribute to overall process performance.
- Process predictability is quantitatively understood.

## Maturity Level 5: Optimizing
At Level 5, the organization continually improves its processes based on a quantitative understanding of business objectives and performance needs:
- Focuses on continuous process improvement through incremental and innovative technological changes.
- Root causes of common variations are identified and addressed systematically.
- Knowledge sharing is institutionalized enterprise-wide.
