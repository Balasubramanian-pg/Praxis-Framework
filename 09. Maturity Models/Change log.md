# Change Log

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Change Log records all formal requests for changes to scope and baselined products, tracking them through change control.

## Purpose
The **Change Log** tracks all change requests submitted against approved baselines (scope, schedule, budget, or specifications). It prevents scope creep and ensures every modification is evaluated for impact, cost, and alignment.

## Key Information
- **Change Request ID**: Unique tracker.
- **Date Submitted & Originator**: Stakeholder or team member requesting the change.
- **Description of Change**: Proposed alteration to baseline deliverables.
- **Business Justification**: Reason (regulatory, efficiency, defect fix, scope expansion).
- **Impact Assessment**: Consequences on schedule, cost, risk, and dependencies.
- **Change Authority Decision**: Approved, Rejected, Deferred, or Escalated.
- **Implementation Status**: Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified.

## Governance
Operated in accordance with the [[Control Scope Change]] procedure and [[General Principles of Configuration Management]].
