# Governance Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The overarching framework of decision-making, authority limits, stakeholder accountability, and oversight governing all P3 activities.

## Purpose
The **Governance Process** provides the structural mechanisms, controls, and oversight necessary to ensure projects, programmes, and portfolios achieve their objectives ethically, legally, and efficiently.

## Principles
- **Separation of Direction and Management**: Clear distinction between the Sponsor (direction/ownership) and the Project Manager (day-to-day management).
- **Tolerances & Escalation**: Autonomous operation within agreed boundaries; mandatory escalation when boundaries are threatened.
- **Stage / Tranche Gateways**: Formal approval gates before committing resources to successive phases.
