# Development Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The technical life cycle process responsible for engineering, testing, and constructing the project's deliverables.

## Purpose
The **Development Process** (or Solutions Development) runs in parallel with the [[Delivery Process]]. While the Delivery Process handles project governance and management, the Development Process focuses on the technical creation and engineering of the outputs.

## Core Activities
1. **Requirements Analysis**: Elaborating user stories, technical requirements, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Design & Architecture**: Engineering functional and physical architectures.
3. **Construction & Assembly**: Coding, fabrication, configuration, or authoring.
4. **Testing & Integration**: Verifying deliverables against specifications and quality criteria.
5. **Deployment & Handover**: Commissioning deliverables into the operational environment.

## Relationship to Praxis Method
The Development Process is typically domain-specific (e.g., Agile software development, waterfall engineering, construction protocols) and interfaces seamlessly with the managerial [[Delivery Process]].
