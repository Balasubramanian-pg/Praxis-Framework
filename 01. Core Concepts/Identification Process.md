# Identification Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The initial life cycle process that develops an outline of the project or programme to assess viability before committing major definition investment.

## Purpose
The **Identification Process** is triggered by a [[Mandate]]. Its primary purpose is to establish an initial outline of the project or programme, determine whether it is worthwhile, and secure sponsor commitment to fund the [[Definition Process]].

## Key Activities
1. **Appoint Sponsor & Project Manager**: Establish core initial leadership.
2. **Develop Brief**: Capture background, objectives, initial scope, and high-level business justification.
3. **Draft Outline Business Case**: Preliminary investment appraisal.
4. **Prepare Definition Plan**: Detailed schedule and budget for running the definition phase.
5. **Authorise Definition**: Sponsor formal gateway review to approve or reject further work.

## Key Inputs & Outputs
- **Inputs**: Strategic Mandate, Lessons Log from past projects.
- **Outputs**: Project/Programme [[Brief]], Outline Business Case, [[Definition Plan]].
