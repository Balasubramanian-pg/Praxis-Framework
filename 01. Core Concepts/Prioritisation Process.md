# Prioritisation Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Evaluates candidate projects and programmes against strategic objectives to rank them in order of value, urgency, and resource feasibility.

## Purpose
The **Prioritisation Process** evaluates potential and active components within a portfolio to determine their relative rank and strategic priority.

## Key Activities
1. **Define Scoring Criteria**: Establish weighted strategic metrics (ROI, strategic fit, risk profile, regulatory compliance).
2. **Score & Rank Initiatives**: Assess candidate business cases against the criteria.
3. **Balance Resource Constraints**: Identify bottlenecks in capital, skilled talent, or supply chain.
4. **Recommend Portfolio Composition**: Present prioritized options to the executive portfolio board.

## Outputs
- Prioritized project and programme pipeline.
- Portfolio investment recommendations.
