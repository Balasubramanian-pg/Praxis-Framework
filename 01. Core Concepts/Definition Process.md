# Definition Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The life cycle process where the project or programme is planned in full detail, producing management plans, delivery plans, and the full business case.

## Purpose
The **Definition Process** takes the approved [[Brief]] and elaborates it into a fully articulated, integrated baseline for execution. It answers *what* will be delivered, *how* it will be governed, *when* it will happen, and *how much* it will cost.

## Key Activities
1. **Assemble Management Plans**: Author policies and procedures for Risk, Quality, Scope, Schedule, Finance, Resources, Stakeholders, and Change.
2. **Develop Solution & Specifications**: Engineer technical architectures and detailed product breakdowns.
3. **Prepare Delivery Plans**: Schedule stages/tranches, estimate costs, and balance resources.
4. **Finalize Business Case**: Complete comprehensive investment appraisal and benefits forecasts.
5. **Gateway Review (Gate 2)**: Sponsor and governing body authorize entry into delivery.

## Key Outputs
- Full [[Manage The Business Case|Business Case]].
- All functional [[Management Plans]].
- Master [[Delivery Plan]] and first Stage Plan.
