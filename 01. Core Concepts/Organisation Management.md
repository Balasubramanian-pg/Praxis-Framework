# Organisation Management

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The function that designs, recruits, maintains, and demobilizes the temporary management team and governance structure.

## Overview
**Organisation Management** deals with designing the appropriate organizational structure for a project, programme, or portfolio. Because P3 organizations are temporary, they must be rapidly formed, effectively maintained, and cleanly disbanded.

## Goals
1. Design a management team structure appropriate for the complexity and scale of the work.
2. Clearly define roles, responsibilities, and decision-making limits.
3. Appoint competent individuals and build a cohesive working environment.
4. Smoothly transition team members upon completion.
