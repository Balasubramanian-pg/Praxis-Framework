# Overview of Identification Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> High-level summary of the first phase of the project and programme life cycle.

## Purpose
The **Overview of Identification Process** summarizes the initial gateway where ideas and mandates are transformed into justifiable project briefs.

## Key Goals
- Rapidly determine if the proposed work is viable and aligned with strategy.
- Prevent premature commitment of extensive organizational resources.
- Produce the [[Brief]] and [[Definition Plan]] for Sponsor authorization.
