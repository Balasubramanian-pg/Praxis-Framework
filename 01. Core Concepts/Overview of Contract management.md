# Overview of Contract Management

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> An executive summary of principles and methods for legally binding relationships with suppliers.

## Purpose
**Contract Management** is the management function that oversees the agreement, execution, and formal closure of contracts between the project/programme organization and external suppliers.

## Key Objectives
- Ensure that both parties meet their contractual commitments.
- Manage contractual scope variations through formal change mechanisms.
- Authorize milestone payments upon verified deliverable acceptance.
- Minimize exposure to dispute, arbitration, or commercial loss.
