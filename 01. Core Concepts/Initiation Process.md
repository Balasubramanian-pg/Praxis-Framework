# Initiation Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The portfolio management process that establishes the portfolio's governance infrastructure, strategic scope, and operating model.

## Purpose
The **Initiation Process** sets up a new portfolio or realigns an existing one with corporate strategy. It establishes the organizational structure, resources, and governance rules required to direct projects and programmes consistently.

## Key Activities
1. **Define Portfolio Scope**: Determine which organizational domains, investments, and programmes fall within the portfolio boundary.
2. **Establish Governance Infrastructure**: Setup PMO / support functions, reporting channels, and resource pools.
3. **Draft Portfolio Management Plans**: Tailor framework policies for risk, scheduling, and finance across all components.
4. **Secure Executive Endorsement**: Board sign-off on the portfolio charter.
