# Manage Boundaries Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to govern stage and tranche transitions, conducting gateway reviews and evaluating ongoing viability.

## Purpose
The **Manage Boundaries** competency focuses on the formal review points between stages or tranches where the business case is reassessed before authorizing the next phase.

## Performance Criteria
1. Review stage/tranche performance against baseline plans.
2. Prepare the delivery plan for the upcoming stage or tranche.
3. Update the business case and risk profile with latest actuals.
4. Present evidence at the formal gateway review to secure sponsor approval.
