# Co-ordinate A Portfolio Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to coordinate concurrent projects and programmes, balancing interdependencies and resource pools.

## Purpose
The **Co-ordinate A Portfolio** competency certifies a portfolio manager's ability to maintain cross-initiative balance, resolve competing priorities, and align shared delivery milestones.

## Performance Criteria
1. Monitor interdependencies between projects and programmes within the portfolio.
2. Resolve multi-project resource conflicts and prioritize allocations.
3. Coordinate portfolio-level risk responses and contingency reserves.
4. Maintain aggregate reporting dashboards for executive leadership.
