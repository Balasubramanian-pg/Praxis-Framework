# Vision Statement

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> A concise, inspirational declaration of the future state that the project or programme aims to deliver.

## Purpose
The **Vision Statement** encapsulates the ultimate purpose and transformational ambition of a project or programme. It serves as an emotional and strategic anchor for leaders, teams, and stakeholders.

## Characteristics
- **Clarity**: Unambiguous description of the target operational state.
- **Inspirational**: Motivates stakeholders to navigate complex organizational change.
- **Strategic Alignment**: Directly connects project outputs to enterprise strategy.

## Timing
Authored during the [[Identification Process]] by the Sponsor and senior leaders.
