# Manage A Portfolio Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to direct and govern a portfolio across categorisation, prioritisation, and performance cycles.

## Purpose
The **Manage A Portfolio** competency encompasses the strategic leadership of a portfolio to ensure optimal return on investment.

## Performance Criteria
1. Maintain alignment between the portfolio composition and evolving enterprise strategy.
2. Direct periodic portfolio reviews to re-balance and re-prioritize initiatives.
3. Manage executive stakeholder relationships and board communication.
4. Champion organizational capability maturity and continuous improvement.
