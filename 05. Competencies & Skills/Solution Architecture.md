# Solution Architecture

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The practice of designing comprehensive technical and operational solutions that fulfill the project scope and specifications.

## Overview
**Solution Architecture** translates business requirements into viable, scalable, and resilient technical or operational models. It bridges the gap between the business problem and the engineering implementation.

## Key Objectives
- Ensure alignment between technical designs and the [[Business Case]].
- Evaluate trade-offs between cost, time, complexity, and technical debt.
- Define interfaces, data flows, and architectural constraints.
- Verify feasibility through prototypes and proof-of-concepts during the [[Definition Process]].
