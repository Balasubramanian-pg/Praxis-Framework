# Negotiate Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the ability to reach mutually beneficial agreements with internal and external parties on scope, resources, contracts, and timelines.

## Purpose
The **Negotiate** competency focuses on resolving differences, agreeing on commercial or operational terms, and establishing balanced commitments between two or more parties.

## Performance Criteria
1. Prepare thoroughly by identifying interests, walk-away positions, and BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement).
2. Create an open, collaborative negotiation climate focusing on mutual value.
3. Trade concessions effectively while protecting core project objectives and tolerances.
4. Formulate clear, binding, and documented agreements.
5. Maintain positive professional relationships throughout contentious discussions.

## Knowledge Criteria
- Principled negotiation methodologies (e.g., Harvard Negotiation Project).
- Contract law fundamentals, commercial negotiation terms, and dispute resolution mechanisms.
