# Delegate Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the ability to allocate work, responsibility, and authority effectively while retaining overall accountability.

## Purpose
The purpose of the **Delegate** competency is to enable a manager to allocate tasks and work packages to individuals or teams, providing appropriate authority and resources while maintaining accountability for the overall outcome.

## Performance Criteria
1. Define the scope, objectives, constraints, and quality criteria of the delegated work.
2. Select appropriate individuals or teams based on competence, workload, and development goals.
3. Clarify levels of authority, reporting channels, and tolerances.
4. Provide necessary resources, guidance, and support without micromanaging.
5. Review completed work and provide constructive feedback.

## Knowledge Criteria
- Principles of effective delegation and situational leadership.
- Techniques for defining unambiguous work packages and tolerances.
- Understanding legal, organizational, and contractual constraints on delegation.
