# Influence Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the ability to persuade and align stakeholders and team members to support project objectives without relying solely on formal authority.

## Purpose
The **Influence** competency focuses on building credibility, persuading diverse stakeholders, and fostering alignment in environments where the project manager does not possess direct hierarchical authority.

## Performance Criteria
1. Identify the motivations, priorities, and concerns of key stakeholders.
2. Adapt communication styles and persuasion tactics to suit individual personalities.
3. Construct evidence-based, compelling arguments linking project needs to organizational value.
4. Build coalitions and leverage informal networks to overcome resistance.
5. Negotiate win-win agreements that preserve long-term working relationships.

## Knowledge Criteria
- Theories of social influence, power dynamics, and emotional intelligence.
- Stakeholder analysis frameworks and empathy mapping.
