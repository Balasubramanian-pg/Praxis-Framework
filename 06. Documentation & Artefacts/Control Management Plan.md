# Control Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Defines the policies, reporting cadences, tolerances, and escalation procedures for tracking and controlling delivery performance.

## Purpose
The **Control Management Plan** sets out how performance data will be gathered, analyzed, and controlled across the life cycle. It outlines the governance thresholds and mechanisms for keeping work within approved parameters.

## Core Elements
- **Policy**: Defined tolerances for time, cost, quality, scope, benefits, and risk.
- **Tracking Procedures**: Earned Value Management (EVM), milestone tracking, burn-down charts.
- **Reporting Schedule**: Cadence of checkpoint reports, progress reports, and executive summaries.
- **Escalation Rules**: When and how an Exception Report must be raised to the Sponsor.
