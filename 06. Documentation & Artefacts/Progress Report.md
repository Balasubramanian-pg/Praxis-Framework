# Progress Report

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> A periodic, time-driven report from the project or programme manager to the sponsor and governing body summarizing performance.

## Purpose
The **Progress Report** provides periodic visibility into actual progress against the baseline plan. It ensures transparent governance and enables the Sponsor to monitor delivery without micromanagement.

## Key Sections
- **Period Covered & Current RAG Status**: Red/Amber/Green health indicator.
- **Achievements in Period**: Deliverables completed and milestones reached.
- **Planned for Next Period**: Upcoming work packages and key dates.
- **Variance Analysis**: Deviations in cost, schedule, and scope against tolerances.
- **Top Risks & Issues**: Critical threats and escalated issues requiring sponsor attention.
- **Budget / Spend Position**: Actual spend versus forecast.

## Frequency
Typically published weekly, fortnightly, or monthly as dictated by the [[Control Management Plan]].
