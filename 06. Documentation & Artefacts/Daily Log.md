# Daily Log

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> An informal diary maintained by the project or programme manager to record daily events, ideas, minor issues, and reminders.

## Purpose
The **Daily Log** provides an informal, working record for the project or programme manager to note day-to-day events, informal conversations, quick observations, and temporary reminders that do not warrant immediate formal logging in the [[Issue Register]] or [[Risk Register]].

## Content & Characteristics
- **Informal & Dynamic**: Not a public governance document; maintained for managerial recall.
- **Typical Entries**:
  - Notes from informal stakeholder chats or vendor calls.
  - Action items or reminders for upcoming meetings.
  - Initial observations of friction or emerging technical challenges.
  - Minor queries awaiting resolution.
- **Filtering Mechanism**: Items in the Daily Log that develop into significant threats or deviations are escalated into the [[Risk Register]] or [[Issue Register]].

## Timing
- Maintained continuously across the entire life cycle, primarily in the [[Delivery Process]].
