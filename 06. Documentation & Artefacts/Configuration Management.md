# Configuration Management

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Outlines how assets, deliverables, and documents are identified, version-controlled, tracked, and audited.

## Purpose
The **Configuration Management** plan or artefact specifies how product baselines and document versions are maintained and verified across the life cycle.

## Core Activities
1. **Identification**: Assigning unique identifiers to configuration items (CIs).
2. **Control**: Preventing unauthorized changes to baselined items.
3. **Status Accounting**: Recording current state, version, and location of items.
4. **Verification & Audit**: Physical and functional inspections to verify baseline compliance.
