# Follow-on Actions Report

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Documents unresolved issues, ongoing maintenance responsibilities, and residual risks transferred to operational teams upon closure.

## Purpose
The **Follow-on Actions Report** lists all outstanding actions, unresolved minor issues, operational recommendations, and ongoing risks when the project or programme team is demobilized during the [[Closure Process]].

## Key Contents
- **Handover Items**: Operational manuals, warranty terms, and service agreements.
- **Outstanding Deliverables**: Minor defects or secondary enhancements deferred to operational maintenance.
- **Residual Risks**: Threats and opportunities transferred to permanent departmental risk registers.
- **Assigned Stewardship**: Named operational owners for every outstanding action.

## Timing
Authored during the [[Closure Process]] and accepted by the Sponsor and operations leads before formal disbandment.
