# Definition Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> A delivery plan created in the identification process describing how the definition phase will be conducted, resourced, and governed.

## Purpose
The **Definition Plan** sets out the scope, schedule, budget, and resources required to carry out the [[Definition Process]]. It ensures that defining the project is treated with disciplined planning rather than an uncoordinated preliminary effort.

## Key Contents
- **Scope of Definition**: What investigations, feasibility studies, architectures, and business cases need to be produced.
- **Schedule**: Timeline for producing all management plans and the business case.
- **Budget**: Funds required to run the definition phase (consultants, workshops, software trials).
- **Resources**: Assigned team members and specialist contributors.
- **Acceptance Criteria**: Criteria to determine whether the definition phase is complete.

## Timing
- Prepared in the [[Identification Process]] alongside the [[Brief]].
- Approved by the Sponsor to authorize entry into the [[Definition Process]].
