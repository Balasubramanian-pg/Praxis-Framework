# Delivery Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> A comprehensive operational plan detailing the work, schedule, budget, and resources for a specific stage or tranche.

## Purpose
The **Delivery Plan** is the operational master document for executing a defined phase of work. In projects, it is typically a **Stage Plan**; in programmes, it is a **Tranche Plan**; when addressing major deviations, it is an **Exception Plan**.

## Key Sections
1. **Scope Breakdown**: Work packages, deliverables, and specifications.
2. **Schedule**: Gantt charts, network diagrams, milestone schedules, and critical path.
3. **Resource Requirements**: People, facilities, equipment, and third-party contracts.
4. **Budget & Cash Flow**: Cost allocations and expenditure forecast for the period.
5. **Control Tolerances**: Permissible variations in time, cost, scope, and quality.

## Life Cycle Timing
- Authored at the end of each stage in preparation for the next during the [[Delivery Process]].
- Formally approved by the Sponsor before commencing work in the next stage.
