# Manage The Business Case Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to formulate, justify, maintain, and confirm the viability of the project business case.

## Purpose
The **Manage The Business Case** competency ensures that the initiative's ongoing justification is continuously evaluated against changing costs, timescales, risks, and strategic priorities.

## Performance Criteria
1. Formulate the initial Outline Business Case during the [[Identification Process]].
2. Elaborate the comprehensive Full Business Case during the [[Definition Process]].
3. Review and update business case viability at every stage boundary.
4. Advise the Sponsor if viability is compromised, recommending corrective action or premature closure.
