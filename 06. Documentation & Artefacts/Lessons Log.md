# Lessons Log

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Lessons Log captures knowledge and experience gained during the project or programme to improve current and future performance.

## Purpose
The **Lessons Log** captures insights, successes, mistakes, and experiential knowledge gained during the life cycle. It serves as both a repository of wisdom for the ongoing project and an asset for organizational capability maturity.

## Structure
- **Lesson ID & Date**: When recorded.
- **Context / Trigger**: What happened (e.g., supplier delay, estimation shortfall, successful workshop).
- **Analysis**: Root cause of why it happened.
- **Recommendation**: What should be repeated or avoided.
- **Applicability**: Relevant to current stages, upcoming tranches, or future enterprise projects.
- **Status**: Recorded, Verified, Embedded in standard process.

## Life Cycle Timing
- **Initiated**: In the [[Identification Process]] by reviewing lessons from past projects.
- **Updated**: Continuously throughout the [[Delivery Process]] and at end-stage reviews.
- **Finalized**: During the [[Closure Process]] to create the Lessons Learned Report for the host organization.
