# Finance Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Sets out the policy and procedures for budgeting, cash flow forecasting, accounting, and financial control.

## Purpose
The **Finance Management Plan** describes how the financial resources of the project or programme will be budgeted, secured, tracked, and audited throughout the life cycle.

## Core Elements
- **Cost Categorization**: Capital expenditure (CapEx) vs. Operational expenditure (OpEx), direct vs. indirect costs.
- **Estimating Standards**: Bottom-up, parametric, or analogous estimation methodologies.
- **Contingency & Reserves**: Management reserves and contingency funds allocation rules.
- **Financial Controls**: Sign-off limits, invoice approval workflows, and audit requirements.
