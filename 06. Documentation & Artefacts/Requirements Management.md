# Requirements Management Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Capability maturity benchmarks for eliciting, structuring, and baselining scope requirements.

## Overview
Capability maturity benchmarks for **Requirements Management**.

## Levels
- **Level 1**: Poorly documented requirements; frequent scope disputes during testing.
- **Level 2**: Structured elicitation, MoSCoW prioritization, and documented acceptance criteria.
- **Level 3**: End-to-end requirement traceability matrices and standardized validation protocols.
- **Level 4**: Defect density analysis, requirement volatility metrics, and quantitative verification.
- **Level 5**: Continuous customer feedback integration and automated requirement validation.
