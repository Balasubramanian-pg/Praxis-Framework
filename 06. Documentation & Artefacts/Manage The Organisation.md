# Manage The Organisation Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to structure project governance, define managerial roles, and maintain team cohesion.

## Purpose
The **Manage The Organisation** competency demonstrates competence in establishing temporary project teams, setting reporting relationships, and governing managerial delegation.

## Performance Criteria
1. Author the [[Organisation Management Plan]].
2. Design organizational charts reflecting lines of authority between Sponsor, Manager, and Teams.
3. Onboard key team members and manage performance throughout the life cycle.
4. Demobilize the organization gracefully at project completion.
