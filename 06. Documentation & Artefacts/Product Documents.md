# Product Documents

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> A collection of granular specifications, descriptions, and quality criteria for individual products and components.

## Purpose
**Product Documents** assemble detailed information about individual products, sub-assemblies, and deliverables within the Work Breakdown Structure.

## Typical Components
- **Product Description**: Purpose, composition, quality criteria, and tolerances.
- **Quality Register**: Status of quality inspections, tests, and approvals.
- **Configuration Item Record**: Version history, location, and maintenance metadata.

## Usage
Used during the [[Delivery Process]] by delivery teams to guide construction and verification.
