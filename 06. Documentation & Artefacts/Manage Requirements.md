# Manage Requirements Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to capture, document, prioritize, and baseline user and technical requirements.

## Purpose
The **Manage Requirements** competency ensures that all stakeholder expectations are systematically captured, structured, and validated as measurable project requirements.

## Performance Criteria
1. Conduct elicitation workshops, interviews, and prototyping sessions.
2. Document requirements with unambiguous acceptance criteria.
3. Prioritize requirements using MoSCoW (Must, Should, Could, Won't have).
4. Maintain traceability matrices linking requirements to deliverables and business goals.
