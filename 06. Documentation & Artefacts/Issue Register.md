# Issue Register

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The Issue Register records all problems, deviations, and unforeseen events that exceed local tolerance and require formal management resolution.

## Purpose
The **Issue Register** captures, tracks, and documents the resolution of all formal issues. In Praxis, an issue is any event or deviation that has occurred (or is certain to occur) that threatens project objectives and exceeds defined managerial tolerances, necessitating escalation.

## Key Fields
- **Issue ID**: Unique reference.
- **Date Raised & Raised By**: Who detected the issue.
- **Description**: Precise nature of the problem, failure, or unexpected constraint.
- **Impact Assessment**: Impact on cost, schedule, scope, quality, or benefits.
- **Severity / Priority**: Critical, Major, Minor.
- **Owner**: Assigned person responsible for implementing the corrective resolution.
- **Resolution Plan / Actions**: Steps taken or exception plan triggered.
- **Escalation Flag**: Whether escalated to Sponsor via an Exception Report.
- **Current Status**: Open, Investigating, Resolving, Closed.

## Life Cycle Context
- Maintained throughout the [[Delivery Process]].
- Issues that cannot be solved within stage tolerances trigger an **Exception Plan**.
