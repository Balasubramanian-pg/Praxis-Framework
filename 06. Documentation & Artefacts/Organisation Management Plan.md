# Organisation Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Outlines the structure, roles, responsibilities, reporting lines, and appointment processes for the P3 management team.

## Purpose
The **Organisation Management Plan** establishes the temporary governance structure needed to manage and direct the project, programme, or portfolio.

## Key Sections
- **Governance Hierarchy**: Sponsor, Programme/Project Board, Management Team, Team Managers.
- **Role Descriptions**: Detailed terms of reference for all managerial appointments.
- **Staffing & Recruitment**: How human resources are onboarded, inducted, and released.
- **Decision Authority**: Delegated limits of authority and escalation pathways.

## Timing
Created in the [[Definition Process]] by the Project/Programme Manager.
