# Goal of Closure Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Core goals, handover criteria, and success metrics for closing a project or programme.

## Goals of the Closure Process
The primary goals of the **Closure Process** in the Praxis Framework are:
1. **Deliverable Handover**: Formally transfer ownership of outputs and products to the operational teams or client.
2. **Performance Review**: Evaluate project performance against the approved business case, delivery plans, and baseline tolerances.
3. **Capture Lessons**: Record institutional insights, successes, and learning in the [[Lessons Log]].
4. **Demobilise Resources**: Orderly release of personnel, facilities, and physical assets back to the organization.
5. **Contractual & Financial Close**: Finalize supplier accounts, release retentions, and archive records.
