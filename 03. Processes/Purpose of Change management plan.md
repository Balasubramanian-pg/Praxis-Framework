# Purpose of Change Management Plan

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Explains the purpose, scope, and principles of the Change Management Plan.

## Purpose
The **Change Management Plan** documents the strategy for managing the organizational and human transformation required to adopt project deliverables and achieve desired business benefits.

## Distinction in Praxis
- **Scope Change Control**: Handled by [[Control Scope Change]] and [[General Principles of Configuration Management]] to protect product baselines.
- **Organizational Change Management**: Handled by the Change Management Plan to transition business teams, adjust culture, and embed new workflows.

## Key Contents
- Stakeholder impact assessments and readiness surveys.
- Training curricula and transition schedules.
- Change agent networks and operational support mechanisms.
