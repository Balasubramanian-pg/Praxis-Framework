# Overall Goal of Management Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The overarching objectives of day-to-day project and programme management.

## Goals of the Management Process
The **Management Process** executes the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle to:
1. Direct work package execution and oversee delivery teams.
2. Monitor performance indicators (schedule, cost, quality, risk) against approved baselines.
3. Maintain clear, transparent reporting to stakeholders and the sponsor.
4. Exercise corrective control to keep work within authorized tolerances.
5. Escalate deviations that threaten project success via formal exception mechanisms.
