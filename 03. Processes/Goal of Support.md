# Goal of Support

> [!Note] Overview
> The goals and scope of project, programme, and portfolio support offices (PMO).

## Goals of the Support Function
The **Support** function (often embodied by a Project Management Office - PMO) provides specialized administrative, technical, and analytical services to P3 managers. Its goals are to:
1. **Standardize Processes**: Maintain consistent templates, procedures, and quality benchmarks.
2. **Provide Specialist Services**: Deliver scheduling, risk facilitation, configuration auditing, and cost estimating.
3. **Maintain Software & Tooling**: Administer enterprise P3M software, repositories, and dashboards.
4. **Enable Knowledge Transfer**: Foster [[Communities Of Practice]] and curate organizational lessons.
