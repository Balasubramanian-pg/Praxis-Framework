# Overview of Co-ordination Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Summary of portfolio-wide coordination, interdependency management, and resource balancing.

## Purpose
The **Co-ordination Process** is an ongoing portfolio management process that harmonizes concurrent projects and programmes.

## Key Goals
- Resolve inter-project dependencies and scheduling clashes.
- Optimize shared resource pools (capital, specialized personnel, equipment).
- Manage aggregated portfolio risks and systemic vulnerabilities.
- Align project completion dates with strategic enterprise windows.
