# Lead Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the ability to inspire, guide, and motivate teams and stakeholders to achieve common project objectives.

## Purpose
The **Lead** competency focuses on setting clear direction, fostering psychological safety, empowering team members, and sustaining collective motivation through uncertainty and challenges.

## Performance Criteria
1. Articulate an inspiring project vision that connects daily tasks to organizational purpose.
2. Adapt leadership styles (directive, coaching, affiliative, democratic) to situational needs.
3. Foster collaboration, mutual respect, and high ethical standards within the team.
4. Recognize achievements and support individual career and skills development.
5. Maintain resilience and provide calm, decisive direction during crises.
