# Goals of Definition Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Detailed objectives and success criteria for the definition planning phase.

## Goals of the Definition Process
The **Definition Process** establishes the baselines for the entire project or programme. Its primary goals are:
1. **Define Scope & Solutions**: Translate user requirements into technical specifications and work breakdown structures.
2. **Assemble Functional Policies**: Author comprehensive [[Management Plans]] for risk, schedule, finance, and quality.
3. **Build Integrated Delivery Plans**: Formulate resource-balanced schedules and budgets for delivery stages.
4. **Justify the Investment**: Produce a robust, defensible [[Business Case]] with realistic ROI.
5. **Obtain Commitment**: Secure formal sponsor approval and governance sign-off to start execution.
