# Goal of Sponsorship Process

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Strategic aims, executive ownership, and governance gateways operated by the project sponsor.

## Goals of the Sponsorship Process
The sponsorship process connects executive strategy with project execution. Its primary goals are to:
1. **Provide Executive Direction**: Maintain ongoing alignment between the project and corporate strategy.
2. **Own the Business Case**: Ensure that investment expenditure remains justified by expected benefits.
3. **Authorize Gateway Transitions**: Authorize entry into Definition, Delivery, and Closure phases.
4. **Establish Tolerances**: Define managerial boundaries for cost, schedule, risk, and scope.
5. **Resolve Escalations**: Adjudicate issues exceeding the project manager's delegated authority.
