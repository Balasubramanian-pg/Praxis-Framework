# Develop Investment Appraisal Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to evaluate capital investments using financial appraisal techniques like NPV, IRR, and payback period.

## Purpose
The **Develop Investment Appraisal** competency focuses on evaluating the financial viability and business value of proposed initiatives to inform go/no-go decisions.

## Performance Criteria
1. Quantify life cycle costs, capital investments, and ongoing operational costs.
2. Forecast financial and non-financial benefits over the target evaluation period.
3. Calculate Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Payback Period.
4. Conduct sensitivity analysis to assess vulnerability to market and cost fluctuations.
