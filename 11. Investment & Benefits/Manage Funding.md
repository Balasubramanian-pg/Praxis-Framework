# Manage Funding Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to secure, release, and reconcile capital and operational funding across stage gateways.

## Purpose
The **Manage Funding** competency covers obtaining financial tranches from internal sponsors or external investors and managing cash flow liquidity throughout delivery.

## Performance Criteria
1. Identify funding sources, constraints, and release conditions.
2. Prepare cash-flow draw-down schedules aligned with stage boundaries.
3. Reconcile funding disbursements against verified project expenditures.
4. Secure supplementary funding or return surplus funds at project close.
