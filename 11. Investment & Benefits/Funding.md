# Funding Maturity

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Organizational capability maturity benchmarks for securing and managing capital and operational funding.

## Overview
Defines the organizational maturity benchmarks for securing and managing project funding.

## Maturity Benchmarks
- **Level 1**: Chaotic or unpredictable funding releases; work stalls due to cash flow interruptions.
- **Level 2**: Formal funding draw-downs aligned with approved stage gates and delivery plans.
- **Level 3**: Enterprise capital allocation frameworks and multi-year funding portfolios.
- **Level 4**: Real-time cash flow optimization and liquidity modeling across all portfolio components.
- **Level 5**: Dynamic funding reallocation adapting immediately to market opportunities.
