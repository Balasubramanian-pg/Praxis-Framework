# Procurement Overall Goal

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The overarching strategic and operational goals of the procurement function.

## Goals of Procurement
The primary goals of the **Procurement** function in P3 management are to:
1. Obtain products and services that meet quality specifications at a competitive price.
2. Establish balanced, fair, and legally sound contractual agreements.
3. Allocate risks equitably between client and supplier.
4. Maintain productive supplier relationships throughout delivery and contract closure.
