# Realise Benefits Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to transition delivered data products into business-as-usual operations, audit post-implementation user adoption, and quantify empirical financial and operational ROI against pre-project baselines.

## Purpose
The **Realise Benefits** competency certifies that a project manager, business change manager (BCM), or data product owner can guide host business units from initial technical go-live to full financial and operational benefit realization.

In enterprise Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, and Business Intelligence (BI), a mathematically flawless pipeline and an aesthetically polished dashboard generate **zero business value on their own**. Value is created only when operational teams actively alter business decisions, automate manual processes, or decommission legacy spreadsheets based on certified data.

```
                    The Data & BI Benefit Realization Funnel
                    
[Engineering Output]    Certified Gold Star Schema Mart & Executive BI Cockpit
          │
          ▼
[Business Outcome]      Finance & Operations adopt certified semantic layer; Excel deprecated
          │
          ▼
[Quantified Benefit]    450 analyst hours/month saved from manual data reconciliation
          │
          ▼
[Strategic Impact]      1.8% gross margin improvement via daily regional discount visibility
```

## The Four Tangible Categories of Analytics Benefits

Praxis structures data project benefits into four auditable financial and operational categories:

| Benefit Category | Operational Definition | Measurement Metric | Target Verification Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Direct Labor Recovery** | Elimination of repetitive, manual CSV downloads, spreadsheet merges, and manual VLOOKUP reconciliations. | Total analyst hours saved per month $\times$ fully burdened hourly rate. | Pre/post time-motion audit with departmental Business Change Managers. |
| **2. Decision Velocity & Margin** | Faster identification of operational bottlenecks, price elasticity, and discount leakage. | Basis point increase in Net Gross Margin %; reduction in days-sales-outstanding (DSO). | Finance Controller audit of General Ledger quarterly performance. |
| **3. Infrastructure Cost Take-Out** | Decommissioning expensive legacy on-premises data warehouses, proprietary licenses, and redundant SaaS tools. | Net dollar reduction in annual enterprise IT operational expenditure (OpEx). | IT FinOps contract cancellation and cloud migration audit. |
| **4. Regulatory Risk Avoidance** | Automated audit compliance, lineage tracking, and centralized PII classification. | Zero non-compliance audit findings; automated fulfillment of GDPR/CCPA data requests. | Internal Audit & Data Protection Officer (DPO) formal certification. |

## Performance Criteria

An individual certified in this competency demonstrates the following capabilities:

1. **Establish Pre-Project Baseline Metrics**:
   - Audit the current operational state during the [[Identification Process]]—documenting exact hours spent on manual reporting and calculating baseline metric error rates.
   - Establish auditable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) linked directly to the approved [[Business Case]].

2. **Drive Active Operational Adoption**:
   - Partner with embedded departmental Data Champions to conduct hands-on training and "Analyze in Excel" bridge workshops.
   - Monitor platform usage telemetry (Power BI Activity Logs, Snowflake query user histories) to identify departments lagging in adoption.

3. **Systematically Decommission Legacy Shadow IT**:
   - Execute the scheduled deprecation of legacy emailed CSV reports and direct database read-replica access.
   - Secure formal sign-off from operational department heads certifying that business-critical decisions now run exclusively on the certified semantic layer.

4. **Conduct Post-Project Benefit Audits**:
   - Convene post-implementation reviews 90 and 180 days post-go-live to compare actual business outcomes against the original business case.
   - Document realized return on investment (ROI) and submit a formal Benefits Realization Report to the Project Sponsor.

## Flashcard Q&A

- **Q:** When are benefits actually realized in a Data & BI project?
    - **A:** Benefits are realized only after technical handover, when operational business users adopt the certified semantic models, retire manual spreadsheets, and execute faster, more accurate business decisions that positively impact revenue, cost, or compliance.

- **Q:** How can a data team quantify "labor hours saved" as an auditable benefit?
    - **A:** By benchmarking the pre-project weekly hours spent by analysts manually pulling, cleaning, and reconciling spreadsheets against the post-go-live time spent reviewing automated morning dashboards, multiplying the delta by the burdened hourly rate.

- **Q:** Why is dashboard telemetry tracking essential for benefits realization?
    - **A:** Telemetry tracks Monthly Active Users (MAU), query frequency, and report views. If telemetry shows a department has stopped opening a dashboard, the project manager knows planned benefits are at risk of being lost to shadow spreadsheet reversion.
