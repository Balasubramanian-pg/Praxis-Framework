# Manage Benefits Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to identify, define, map, and track the realization of business benefits resulting from project outputs.

## Purpose
The **Manage Benefits** competency ensures that the project remains focused on delivering measurable strategic value, not merely physical outputs.

## Performance Criteria
1. Identify tangible, intangible, and financial benefits through stakeholder workshops.
2. Construct Benefits Maps linking outputs to business outcomes and strategic goals.
3. Create Benefit Profiles specifying measurement methods, baselines, and targets.
4. Track benefit achievement and assign operational benefit owners.
