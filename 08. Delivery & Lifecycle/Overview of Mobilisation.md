# Overview of Mobilisation

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> An executive summary of the activities required to transition from definition to active delivery.

## Purpose
**Mobilisation** ensures that everything required to execute the work (infrastructure, personnel, IT environments, contracts, and facilities) is fully operational before work starts.

## Key Goals
- Prevent false starts by verifying that team members have tools, access, and instructions.
- Establish communications infrastructure, reporting templates, and repositories.
- Formalize supply contracts and onboarding schedules.
