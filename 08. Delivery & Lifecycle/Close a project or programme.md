# Close A Project Or Programme

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> The structured process for demobilising teams, handing over deliverables, capturing lessons, and archiving records.

## Overview
**Close a project or programme** is the final life cycle process. It ensures that initiatives finish with precision, deliverables are transitioned to operational stewardship, and team members are redeployed respectfully.

## Core Activities
1. **Deliverable Handover**: Obtain formal customer/user acceptance of all deliverables.
2. **Post-Project Review**: Compare final cost, time, and quality against baseline forecasts.
3. **Lessons Learned**: Compile the final [[Lessons Log]] to enrich organizational knowledge.
4. **Demobilisation**: Reassign team members and release equipment.
5. **Follow-on Actions**: Transfer ongoing risks and warranties in the [[Follow-on Actions Report]].
