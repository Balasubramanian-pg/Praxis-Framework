# What Does Control Mean

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Exploration of the philosophy, principles, and practice of managerial control in P3M.

## What Does Control Mean?
In the Praxis Framework, **Control** is not about micro-management or restricting autonomy. It is the disciplined mechanism by which a management team:
1. **Compares Actuals to Plans**: Tracking time, cost, scope, and quality against authorized baselines.
2. **Operates Within Tolerances**: Exercising freedom to manage without seeking permission as long as forecasts remain within boundaries.
3. **Takes Timely Action**: Applying corrective measures early when variances emerge.
4. **Escalates Transparently**: Alerting the Sponsor as soon as a tolerance breach is forecast.
