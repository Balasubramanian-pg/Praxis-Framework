# Identify A Project Or Programme Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to transform mandates into project briefs and evaluate preliminary viability.

## Purpose
The **Identify A Project Or Programme** competency certifies the skills needed to lead the initial identification phase.

## Performance Criteria
1. Interpret strategic mandates and stakeholder triggers.
2. Formulate the Project or Programme [[Brief]].
3. Draft the initial Outline Business Case.
4. Prepare the [[Definition Plan]] and present the gateway recommendation to the Sponsor.
