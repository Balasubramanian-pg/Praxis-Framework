# Work Within A Team

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the ability to collaborate effectively, communicate openly, and contribute productively as a member of a multidisciplinary team.

## Purpose
The **Work Within A Team** competency describes the attitudes and actions required of team members to build cohesion, share accountability, and deliver collective commitments.

## Performance Criteria
1. Understand and fulfill assigned roles and responsibilities within the team.
2. Share information, insights, and risks openly with peers and managers.
3. Support colleagues and resolve minor conflicts constructively.
4. Adhere to agreed team norms, working standards, and quality criteria.
