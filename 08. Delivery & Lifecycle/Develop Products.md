# Develop Products Competency

> [!SUMMARY] Overview
> Demonstrates the capability to execute technical work packages to build and verify required outputs.

## Purpose
The **Develop Products** competency certifies team managers and technical leads in executing assigned work packages to produce quality-approved deliverables.

## Performance Criteria
1. Accept work packages from the project manager, clarifying scope and constraints.
2. Direct technical fabrication, programming, or construction activities.
3. Apply quality control inspections and tests in line with product descriptions.
4. Deliver completed products and transfer quality records to the project manager.
